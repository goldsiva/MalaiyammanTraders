About Us

Malaiyamman Traders – Crackers & Fireworks is your trusted destination for premium fireworks and celebration essentials. Based in Virudhunagar, Tamil Nadu, we’ve been lighting up festivals and special occasions for nearly two decades with safe, high‑quality products and friendly service.

Our shop offers a wide range of crackers — from traditional sparklers and flower pots to vibrant rockets and fancy assortments — all sourced from reputed manufacturers who prioritize safety and brilliance. Whether it’s Diwali, New Year, weddings, or temple festivals, we make sure every celebration shines brighter.

Meet Our Owner

Mr. Gowri Sankar, the proud owner of Malaiyamman Traders, brings over 17 years of experience in the fireworks industry. His deep knowledge of product quality, safety standards, and customer preferences has helped build a loyal community of happy customers across Kerala and Tamil Nadu. Under his guidance, the shop has grown from a small local outlet into a trusted name known for reliability, transparency, and festive spirit.
